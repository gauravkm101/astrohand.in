Aaj Ka Panchang

The five limbs of the day — tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga and karana — computed for your city and read at that city's real sunrise, along with the muhurat windows people actually plan around.

What the Panchang shows

  • Tithi — the lunar day, read at sunrise, which is how a tithi is conventionally assigned to a date.
  • Vara — the weekday.
  • Nakshatra — the lunar mansion the Moon occupies.
  • Yoga and Karana — the two remaining limbs, both computed from the Sun–Moon relationship.
  • Sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset — for your city, not a fixed national time.
  • Rahu Kaal, Gulika Kaal and Yamaghanta — the inauspicious windows.
  • Brahma, Abhijit and Vijaya Muhurta — the auspicious ones.

Why the timings change with your city

Every muhurat window is measured from sunrise. The day from sunrise to sunset is divided into eight equal parts, and the inauspicious part is a different one for each weekday — so Rahu Kaal moves every single day, and it also shifts through the year as sunrise moves. Because it is anchored to sunrise, it is different in Delhi and in Chennai on the same date. A Panchang that prints the same Rahu Kaal for every city and every day is not computing it.

Rahu Kaal by weekday

The eighth of the day that Rahu Kaal falls in is fixed by the weekday: the 8th part on Sunday, the 2nd on Monday, the 7th on Tuesday, the 5th on Wednesday, the 6th on Thursday, the 4th on Friday and the 3rd on Saturday. What changes daily is where those eighths actually fall on the clock, because sunrise and sunset move.

Frequently asked

What is Rahu Kaal and should I avoid it?

It is one eighth of the daylight span, different for each weekday, traditionally avoided for starting anything new. Many people simply avoid it for auspicious beginnings and carry on with routine work.

Why is the tithi read at sunrise?

A tithi can begin or end at any moment of the day. The convention is to name the day after the tithi running at sunrise, which is why sunrise for your own city is what the calculation needs.

Does the Panchang work for cities outside India?

Sunrise and sunset are computed from coordinates, so the limbs and the muhurat windows work anywhere the sun rises and sets.

What is Abhijit Muhurta?

The eighth of the fifteen muhurtas of daylight, straddling local midday. It is broadly considered auspicious for beginnings.
