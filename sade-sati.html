The three phases

  • Rising phase — Saturn in the 12th from your Moon. Traditionally the phase of endings and of quiet pressure building.
  • Peak phase — Saturn over your Moon sign itself. The phase most people mean when they talk about Sade Sati.
  • Setting phase — Saturn in the 2nd from your Moon. The phase of consolidation as the transit moves off.

How it is worked out

Your natal Moon sign comes from your birth details. Saturn's current sign comes from its real position today. If Saturn is in the 12th, the 1st or the 2nd sign from your Moon, Sade Sati is running and the phase follows from which of the three it is. If Saturn is in the 4th or the 8th from your Moon, that is not Sade Sati but Dhaiya, the smaller two-and-a-half-year transit.

What Sade Sati is not

Saturn passes this way about once every thirty years, so most people live through it two or three times. It has a reputation for hardship, and the classical reading is about pressure, responsibility and slow work rather than disaster. Treating it as a sentence is neither accurate to the texts nor useful.

Frequently asked

How long does Sade Sati last?

About seven and a half years — roughly two and a half years in each of the three signs, though Saturn's retrograde motion can stretch the edges.

Is Sade Sati always bad?

The classical description is of a demanding period rather than a ruinous one. Its effect is read alongside Saturn's strength and its role in your own chart.

What is Dhaiya?

The two-and-a-half-year transit of Saturn through the 4th or 8th sign from your Moon. It is often called the small panoti and is not the same as Sade Sati.

Does Sade Sati depend on my sun sign?

No. It is measured from the Moon sign, which is why your birth details are needed rather than just your date.
