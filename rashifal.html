Moon sign, not sun sign

Western horoscopes go by the sun sign. Vedic astrology reads the rashi — the sign the Moon occupied when you were born. The two systems also use different zodiacs: Vedic uses the sidereal zodiac, which currently sits about 24 degrees behind the tropical one. That is why people often find their "star sign" changes when they first look up their Vedic chart. If you do not know your rashi, generate your Kundli first and it will tell you.

How to use a Rashifal honestly

A Rashifal is written for a whole sign, which means roughly one person in twelve. It is a lens for reflection, not a forecast of events, and no reading here is a substitute for a decision you need to make with real information. The parts of this site that are genuinely specific to you — your chart, your doshas, your dasha — come from your own birth details, not from your rashi alone.

Frequently asked

What is the difference between rashi and sun sign?

Rashi is your Moon sign in the sidereal zodiac; the sun sign is the Sun's position in the tropical zodiac. Different body, different zodiac — so they frequently differ.

How do I find my rashi?

Generate your free Kundli with your birth date, time and place. The Moon sign shown there is your rashi.

Is the Rashifal available in Hindi?

Yes, and in the other supported Indian languages too.

Is the Rashifal personalised to my chart?

No — it is written for your rashi, which you share with about one person in twelve. For a reading built from your own chart, use the Kundli.
