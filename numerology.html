The three numbers

  • Life Path — reduced from the digits of your date of birth. The number most systems treat as the central one.
  • Destiny (or Expression) — from every letter of your full name.
  • Soul Urge — from the vowels of your name alone.

Master numbers

Most numbers reduce to a single digit from 1 to 9. Three do not: 11, 22 and 33 are kept as they are and read as master numbers. If your total reduces to one of these, it is not reduced further.

If your name is not in English letters

Pythagorean numerology is defined on the letters A to Z, so a name written in Devanagari, Bangla, Tamil or another Indian script has to be transliterated before it can be scored. That is done here, and the Latin spelling that was actually used is shown back to you — so if you normally spell your name differently in English, you can enter that spelling instead and get the numbers that match it. Where a script cannot be transliterated, the tool says so rather than producing a number from whatever letters happened to survive.

Frequently asked

Which number matters most?

Most systems treat the Life Path as the primary one, because it comes from the date of birth and cannot change. The name-based numbers shift if the spelling of your name changes.

My name in Hindi gives a different number than in English. Why?

Because the numbers are counted from letters, and a transliteration is one of several possible English spellings. Use the spelling you actually go by.

What are 11, 22 and 33?

Master numbers. They are not reduced to a single digit and are read as intensified versions of 2, 4 and 6.

Is numerology part of Vedic astrology?

They are separate systems, though they are often consulted together in India. Your Kundli is computed from planetary positions; your numerology comes from your name and date.
