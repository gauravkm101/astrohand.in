What your Kundli includes

  • Lagna (D1) chart — the ascendant and all nine grahas placed in the twelve houses.
  • Navamsa (D9) chart — the divisional chart traditionally read for marriage and for the deeper strength of a planet.
  • Chandra chart — the same placements counted from your Moon sign.
  • Nakshatra and pada — the lunar mansion your Moon occupied at birth, and its ruling planet.
  • Doshas — Mangal, Kaal Sarp, Pitra and Sade Sati, each checked against the classical rule rather than assumed.
  • Vimshottari dasha — the full 120-year sequence, starting from your Moon-nakshatra lord with the balance at birth, and which period is running now.

How the chart is calculated

Positions are computed from orbital mechanics and then corrected to the sidereal zodiac using the Lahiri ayanamsa, which is the standard Indian government-adopted ayanamsa. The ascendant is derived from local sidereal time using the coordinates of your birth city, which is why the birth place and the birth time both change the result. Nothing in the chart is derived from arithmetic on the digits of your birth date.

Why birth time matters

The Moon moves roughly thirteen degrees a day and the ascendant changes sign about every two hours. A birth time that is wrong by a few hours will usually still give the right Moon sign, but it can easily give the wrong Lagna — and the Lagna decides which house every planet falls in. If you do not know your exact time, the chart is still worth reading, but treat the house positions as approximate.

Frequently asked

Is the Kundli really free?

Yes. Making the chart, reading your doshas, your nakshatra and the dasha timeline costs nothing and there is no limit on how many you make. A paid plan adds the AI written reading and the downloadable PDF report.

Can I get my Kundli in Hindi?

Yes. Choose your language before generating, and the reading can also be translated afterwards into any of the supported Indian languages.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Enter the closest time you know. The Moon sign, nakshatra and dasha will usually still be right; the ascendant and the house placements are the parts that shift with time.

Is this the same as a Kundli made by an astrologer?

The calculations are the same ones a jyotishi works from — the same ayanamsa, the same classical rules for the doshas and the dasha. What a human astrologer adds is judgement about how the factors combine for you personally.
