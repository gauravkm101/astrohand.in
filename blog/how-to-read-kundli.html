How to Read a Kundli: Houses, Planets, and Signs

8 min read · AstroHand Editorial

A Kundli, or Vedic birth chart, can look intimidating at first — a diagram full of numbers, symbols, and lines. But once you understand its three basic building blocks, the chart starts to tell a coherent story. Those three blocks are the houses, the signs, and the planets.

The Twelve Houses (Bhavas)

The birth chart is divided into twelve sections called houses, and each governs a specific area of life. Think of the houses as the "stage" on which the drama of your life unfolds.

The first house represents the self — your personality, appearance, and how you approach life. The second house governs wealth, family, and speech. The third house rules courage, siblings, and communication. The fourth house relates to home, mother, and inner happiness. The fifth house covers creativity, children, and intelligence. The sixth house deals with health, enemies, and daily work.

The seventh house governs marriage and partnerships. The eighth house relates to transformation, longevity, and hidden matters. The ninth house rules fortune, higher learning, and spirituality. The tenth house covers career, status, and public life. The eleventh house governs gains, income, and friendships. And the twelfth house relates to loss, expenses, spirituality, and liberation.

The Signs and Planets

Each house is occupied by one of the twelve zodiac signs (Rashis), which colors how that area of life is expressed. And within the houses sit the planets, each carrying its own energy.

The Sun represents the soul, ego, and vitality. The Moon governs the mind and emotions. Mars rules energy, courage, and drive. Mercury governs intellect and communication. Jupiter represents wisdom, growth, and fortune. Venus rules love, beauty, and comfort. Saturn governs discipline, patience, and hard lessons. Rahu and Ketu, the lunar nodes, relate to worldly desires and spiritual detachment respectively.

Putting It Together

Reading a chart is about combining these three layers. You ask: which planet is in which sign, sitting in which house? For example, Jupiter (wisdom and fortune) in the tenth house (career) suggests a person who may find growth and respect through their profession, perhaps in teaching, advising, or guidance roles. The sign Jupiter sits in then refines how that plays out.

The Ascendant (Lagna)

One of the most important points in the chart is the Ascendant, or Lagna — the sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at your birth. The Lagna sets the entire framework of the chart, determining which sign rules your first house and how all the other houses are arranged. Because the Ascendant changes roughly every two hours, your exact birth time is essential for an accurate chart.

Start Simple

You do not need to master everything at once. Begin by finding your Ascendant, your Moon sign, and your Sun sign. Notice which houses your key planets fall in. Even this basic reading can reveal meaningful themes. Deeper analysis — divisional charts, planetary strengths, and dashas — can come later as your understanding grows.
