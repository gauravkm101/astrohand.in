The 12 Rashis: A Complete Guide to the Vedic Zodiac Signs

9 min read · By AstroHand Editorial Team · Updated 2026-07-23

The twelve rashis are the signs of the Vedic zodiac, the same familiar band from Aries to Pisces that the whole astrological world shares — but read here through the sidereal lens and given a distinctly Jyotish meaning. Each rashi is thirty degrees of the ecliptic, each carries an element, a quality, a ruling planet, and a temperament, and together they form the alphabet from which every birth chart is spelled.

A note before we begin: in Vedic astrology, when someone speaks of "your rashi," they usually mean your Moon rashi — the sign the Moon occupied at your birth — not your Sun sign. The Moon governs the mind, and Jyotish considers your mental and emotional nature the truest starting point for understanding you. So as you read the descriptions below, look up your Moon sign, not just the Sun sign you may know from Western horoscopes.

The Four Elements and Three Qualities

Before the individual signs, two organising patterns help. The signs cycle through four elements — Fire (drive, spirit), Earth (stability, practicality), Air (intellect, relationship), and Water (emotion, intuition) — in a repeating sequence. They also cycle through three qualities: movable/cardinal (initiating), fixed (sustaining), and dual/mutable (adapting). A sign's blend of element and quality is the quickest key to its temperament.

Aries (Mesha) — The Pioneer Ruled by **Mars**, a movable fire sign. Aries is initiative in its purest form: bold, direct, competitive, quick to begin and impatient with delay. Mesha natives lead from the front and thrive on challenge, though they must learn patience and follow-through.

Taurus (Vrishabha) — The Builder Ruled by **Venus**, a fixed earth sign. Taurus values stability, comfort, beauty, and the tangible fruits of steady effort. Reliable and sensual, Vrishabha natives can be immovable once settled — a strength that occasionally hardens into stubbornness.

Gemini (Mithuna) — The Communicator Ruled by **Mercury**, a dual air sign. Curious, verbal, quick-witted, and endlessly adaptable, Mithuna natives live through ideas and exchange. Their gift is versatility; their lesson is depth and consistency amid so many interests.

Cancer (Karka) — The Nurturer Ruled by the **Moon**, a movable water sign. Emotional, protective, deeply attached to home and family, Karka natives feel the world before they analyse it. Their sensitivity is both their compassion and their vulnerability.

Leo (Simha) — The Sovereign Ruled by the **Sun**, a fixed fire sign. Dignified, warm, generous, and hungry for meaningful recognition, Simha natives are natural leaders and performers. Their challenge is to lead from the heart rather than the ego.

Virgo (Kanya) — The Analyst Ruled by **Mercury**, a dual earth sign. Precise, discerning, service-minded, and devoted to improvement, Kanya natives notice the detail everyone else missed. Their perfectionism serves others beautifully but can turn into self-criticism.

Libra (Tula) — The Harmoniser Ruled by **Venus**, a movable air sign. Relational, fair-minded, aesthetic, and diplomatic, Tula natives seek balance and partnership. They weigh every side of a question — a strength that sometimes becomes indecision.

Scorpio (Vrishchika) — The Alchemist Ruled by **Mars** (with Ketu as co-significator), a fixed water sign. Intense, private, penetrating, and transformative, Vrishchika natives live at emotional depths others avoid. They are capable of profound regeneration and fierce loyalty.

Sagittarius (Dhanu) — The Seeker Ruled by **Jupiter**, a dual fire sign. Optimistic, philosophical, freedom-loving, and principled, Dhanu natives are drawn to meaning, teaching, travel, and truth. Their expansiveness can occasionally overlook the practical detail.

Capricorn (Makara) — The Achiever Ruled by **Saturn**, a movable earth sign. Disciplined, ambitious, patient, and responsible, Makara natives climb steadily toward long-term goals. They mature early and endure — Saturn rewards their persistence with lasting results.

Aquarius (Kumbha) — The Visionary Ruled by **Saturn** (with Rahu as co-significator), a fixed air sign. Independent, humanitarian, unconventional, and future-oriented, Kumbha natives think in systems and serve the collective. Their detachment can read as aloofness.

Pisces (Meena) — The Mystic Ruled by **Jupiter**, a dual water sign. Compassionate, imaginative, spiritual, and porous to the feelings of others, Meena natives dissolve boundaries between self and world. Their empathy is boundless; their lesson is discernment.

Why the Ruling Planet Matters

Each rashi is "owned" by a planet, and that planet's condition in your chart strongly colours how the sign expresses. If Venus rules your Moon sign (Taurus or Libra) and Venus is well placed, the graceful, relational qualities of those signs flourish. If the ruler is weak or afflicted, the sign's shadow side comes forward more easily. This is why a full reading never stops at "you are a Taurus" — it asks *how is the lord of that sign doing?*

Reading Yourself Through the Rashis

The rashis are a language, not a set of boxes. Most people are a blend: your Moon sign gives your emotional temperament, your Lagna gives your outward style, and your Sun gives your core will and vitality. When all three differ — as they often do — the tension and combination between them is precisely what makes you individual.

Use the descriptions above as a starting mirror, not a verdict. Look up your Moon rashi and Nakshatra with a real computed chart, notice which planets sit in that sign, and let the picture build in layers. Astrology at its best does not flatten you into a type; it hands you a richer vocabulary for the person you already are.
