The 12 Rashis Explained: Your Complete Zodiac Sign Guide

8 min read · AstroHand Editorial

In Vedic astrology, the zodiac is divided into twelve signs called Rashis. Each Rashi spans 30 degrees of the sky and carries its own personality, ruling planet, and elemental nature. While your Moon sign (the Rashi where the Moon sat at your birth) is considered especially important in Jyotish, understanding all twelve signs helps you read any chart with more depth.

The Four Elements

Every Rashi belongs to one of four elements, which shape its basic temperament. Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) are energetic, passionate, and driven. Earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) are practical, grounded, and reliable. Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) are intellectual, communicative, and social. Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) are emotional, intuitive, and sensitive.

The Twelve Signs

Aries (Mesha) — Ruled by Mars, this fire sign is bold, energetic, and pioneering. Aries natives are natural initiators who love a challenge and dislike waiting.

Taurus (Vrishabha) — Ruled by Venus, this earth sign values stability, comfort, and beauty. Taurus natives are patient, dependable, and deeply loyal, though they can be stubborn.

Gemini (Mithuna) — Ruled by Mercury, this air sign is curious, quick-witted, and communicative. Gemini natives love learning, talking, and variety.

Cancer (Karka) — Ruled by the Moon, this water sign is nurturing, emotional, and protective. Cancer natives are deeply connected to home and family.

Leo (Simha) — Ruled by the Sun, this fire sign is confident, warm, and generous. Leo natives have natural leadership and a flair for the dramatic.

Virgo (Kanya) — Ruled by Mercury, this earth sign is analytical, precise, and service-minded. Virgo natives notice details others miss and strive for improvement.

Libra (Tula) — Ruled by Venus, this air sign seeks balance, harmony, and fairness. Libra natives are diplomatic, charming, and relationship-oriented.

Scorpio (Vrishchika) — Ruled by Mars, this water sign is intense, passionate, and deeply perceptive. Scorpio natives feel things profoundly and value loyalty.

Sagittarius (Dhanu) — Ruled by Jupiter, this fire sign is optimistic, adventurous, and philosophical. Sagittarius natives love freedom, travel, and big ideas.

Capricorn (Makara) — Ruled by Saturn, this earth sign is disciplined, ambitious, and responsible. Capricorn natives are patient climbers who build lasting success.

Aquarius (Kumbha) — Ruled by Saturn, this air sign is original, humanitarian, and independent. Aquarius natives think ahead of their time and value freedom.

Pisces (Meena) — Ruled by Jupiter, this water sign is compassionate, imaginative, and spiritual. Pisces natives are gentle dreamers with deep empathy.

How to Use This

Remember that no one is defined by a single sign. Your full chart weaves together your Moon sign, rising sign (Lagna), Sun sign, and the placements of all nine planets. A Taurus Moon with an Aries rising and a Gemini Sun will feel quite different from a "pure" Taurus. Reading a chart is about seeing how all these energies blend together into a unique individual.
