Lucky Number by Date of Birth: Find Your Moolank and Bhagyank

7 min read · By AstroHand Editorial Team · Updated 2026-07-24

Every date of birth carries a hidden pair of numbers that Vedic numerology treats as a fingerprint of your nature and your destiny. The first is your Moolank (root number, or birth number), and the second is your Bhagyank (destiny number). Calculating them yourself takes less than a minute, and understanding what they mean gives you a simple, practical lens on your temperament, your strengths, and the numbers, colours and days that tend to favour you.

Moolank — your birth number

Your Moolank comes from the day of the month you were born, reduced to a single digit from 1 to 9. If the date is already a single digit, that is your Moolank; if it has two digits, add them until one remains.

- Born on the 5th → Moolank is 5.
- Born on the 14th → 1 + 4 = 5.
- Born on the 29th → 2 + 9 = 11 → 1 + 1 = 2.

The Moolank speaks most strongly about your outward personality — how you naturally act, what draws you, and the first impression you make on others.

Bhagyank — your destiny number

Your Bhagyank comes from the whole date of birth — day, month and year — added together and reduced to a single digit. For someone born on 14 August 1996:

- Day: 1 + 4 = 5
- Month: 8
- Year: 1 + 9 + 9 + 6 = 25 → 2 + 5 = 7
- Total: 5 + 8 + 7 = 20 → 2 + 0 = 2

Where the Moolank describes your nature, the Bhagyank points to the larger arc of your life — the lessons, opportunities and direction that unfold over time. When the two numbers are friendly, life tends to feel aligned; when they clash, there is often an inner tension to reconcile.

The nine numbers and their ruling planets

In Vedic numerology each single digit is governed by a planet and inherits its character:

- 1 — Sun: leadership, individuality, confidence, a need to stand out.
- 2 — Moon: sensitivity, intuition, cooperation, emotional depth.
- 3 — Jupiter: wisdom, optimism, teaching, expansion and growth.
- 4 — Rahu: unconventional thinking, discipline, sudden change, hard work.
- 5 — Mercury: communication, adaptability, quickness, love of freedom.
- 6 — Venus: beauty, relationships, comfort, artistic and material refinement.
- 7 — Ketu: introspection, spirituality, research, a mystical streak.
- 8 — Saturn: responsibility, endurance, ambition, slow but lasting success.
- 9 — Mars: energy, courage, drive, leadership through action.

Reading the planet behind your number often explains your temperament more vividly than the number alone.

Your lucky number, colour and day

Numerology pairs each birth number with favourable numbers, colours and days, drawn from friendly planets:

- 1 (Sun): numbers 1 and 4; colours gold, orange, yellow; day Sunday.
- 2 (Moon): numbers 2 and 7; colours white, cream, silver; day Monday.
- 3 (Jupiter): numbers 3, 6, 9; colours yellow, gold; day Thursday.
- 4 (Rahu): numbers 4 and 8; colours grey, blue; day Saturday.
- 5 (Mercury): numbers 5 and 6; colour green; day Wednesday.
- 6 (Venus): numbers 6 and 9; colours white, pink, pastels; day Friday.
- 7 (Ketu): numbers 2 and 7; colours light greens, smoky shades; day Monday.
- 8 (Saturn): numbers 8 and 4; colours dark blue, black; day Saturday.
- 9 (Mars): numbers 9 and 3; colours red, coral; day Tuesday.

These are traditionally used to choose auspicious dates, phone or vehicle numbers, and the timing of important beginnings — small alignments that many people find reassuring.

Reading the two numbers together

Numerology becomes genuinely useful when you read the pair. If your Moolank and Bhagyank are the same, that quality is doubled — a clear, focused personality, for better and for worse. If they are friendly numbers (for example 1 and 3, both forward-moving), your instincts and your life direction tend to pull the same way. If they clash — say a restless 5 with a slow, structured 8 — you may feel torn between what you want in the moment and where life keeps steering you, and much of your growth lies in reconciling the two. Numerologists also watch the name number, derived from the letters of your name; unlike a birth date it can be adjusted, and is sometimes tuned to bring greater harmony with the birth numbers.

A note of honesty

Numerology is a symbolic system, not a mechanical predictor of events. Your birth numbers describe tendencies and inclinations — they do not fix your fate, and no lucky number replaces effort, judgement and character. Used well, it is a mirror for self-understanding and a gentle guide for timing, best read alongside your full astrological chart rather than in isolation.
