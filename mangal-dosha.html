The rule this uses

Mangal Dosha is present when Mars occupies the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house, counted from the Lagna or from the Moon. Both reference points are checked, because a chart can be clean from one and afflicted from the other. The result names the house, so you can see the reasoning rather than a bare yes or no.

Why so many people come out Manglik

Six of twelve houses count, and the count is taken from two different reference points — so a large share of charts qualify under the strict rule. That is a property of the rule, not a sign that something is unusually wrong with your chart. Classical practice reads the strength and the dignity of Mars, and the rest of the chart, before treating it as a serious matter.

Cancellation

Traditional texts list a number of conditions under which the dosha is considered cancelled or reduced — among them both partners carrying it, Mars in its own or exalted sign, and certain aspects from benefics. This is the point at which a real astrologer is worth more than a calculator.

Frequently asked

Can a Manglik marry a non-Manglik?

It is a common question and the classical answer is not a flat no — the texts describe several cancelling conditions. Most practitioners look at the strength of Mars and the whole chart rather than the label alone.

Is Mangal Dosha checked from the Moon as well?

Yes. Counting only from the ascendant misses charts that are afflicted from the Moon, which is why both are used here.

Does Mangal Dosha end at a certain age?

Some traditions hold that its effect reduces with age. This is a matter of interpretation rather than calculation, and opinions differ.
